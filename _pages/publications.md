### On the Rate Region of I.I.D. Discrete Signaling and Treating Interference as Noise for the Gaussian Broadcast Channel

- Venue: IEEE ISIT 2026 (accepted)
- Date: 2026-03-28
- Link: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04092)

We revisit the Gaussian broadcast channel (GBC) and explore the rate region achieved by purely discrete inputs with treating interference as noise (TIN) decoding.

Specifically, we introduce a simple scheme based on superposition coding with identically and independently distributed inputs drawn from discrete constellations, for example pulse amplitude modulations (PAM).

Most importantly, we prove that the resulting achievable rate region under TIN decoding is within a constant gap to the capacity region of the GBC.
